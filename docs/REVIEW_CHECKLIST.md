This is a synthetic local demonstration. A practitioner review validates workflow and terminology; it does not certify any supplier, real tender outcome, or legal compliance.

## Review the included case

1. Open the document manifest and confirm every file is synthetic, hashed, and unchanged.
2. Open every criterion's citation. Confirm published wording, stage, mandatory status, and required evidence.
3. Confirm the 24-month requirement T01 is superseded by the cited 18-month addendum T01A.
4. Confirm methods and expected values reflect only the cited requirements. For a model draft, inspect omissions and select **Configure check** beside each criterion: choose the check, submitted evidence, and exact published value. For price arithmetic, enter the published currency and rounding increment. Saving creates a new revision; inspect it before locking. Advanced criterion editing remains available for corrections to stage, mandatory status, and addendum links.
5. Enter your own named plan approval after review. A TEST SIMULATION approval does not satisfy this step.
6. Evaluate, inspect matrix sources, and verify the known fixture results below.
7. Inspect price calculations and the reference-source queue. A failed portal lookup must remain unresolved.
8. Confirm committee recommendation, signatures, and award decision are blank. Approve export only after reviewing this exact draft.
9. Open the exported package offline and check its original hashes, citations, approval records, and complete tool log.
